Refund Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

This policy explains when a refund is available for paid Våld Labs digital products, what is normally not refundable, the statutory rights that always apply, and how to make a request.

Våld Labs is the brand name under which Jaime Paiva, an independent software developer based in Lisbon, Portugal, develops and sells these products. Våld Labs is not a separate legal entity. Full legal details are in section 10.

1. Scope

This policy applies to paid Våld Labs digital products purchased through valdlabs.com, where payment is processed by Lemon Squeezy.

Purchases made through the Apple App Store or another marketplace follow that platform's refund process and are not covered by this policy; please contact the platform directly.

Nothing in this policy restricts the mandatory rights you have as a consumer under the law of your country of residence. Where this policy and a mandatory right differ, the mandatory right applies.

2. Merchant of record

Lemon Squeezy acts as merchant of record for purchases on valdlabs.com. It processes payments and applicable taxes, issues invoices, carries out approved refunds and handles chargebacks.

You can contact Våld Labs at vald.labs.lisbon@gmail.com for help with any order. We assess refund requests and coordinate approved refunds with Lemon Squeezy.

3. Before purchasing

Downloadable software cannot be handed back once it has been delivered, so please check before buying that a product fits your setup. In particular, review:

  • the demonstrations, screenshots and videos on the product page;
  • the system requirements;
  • the supported operating systems and minimum versions;
  • the processor requirements (for example, Apple silicon, Intel or x64);
  • the plugin formats (for example, VST3, AU or standalone);
  • DAW or host compatibility; and
  • any free beta, demo or trial version available for the product.

If compatibility is unclear, contact us before purchasing and we will do our best to answer.

4. Eligible refund situations

A refund may be provided where:

  • you were charged more than once for the same order;
  • payment was completed but the download or licence cannot be delivered;
  • the product has a material technical defect on a supported system and we cannot provide a reasonable fix or workaround;
  • the product is materially different from its published description;
  • an unauthorised or fraudulent transaction is confirmed; or
  • applicable law requires a refund or another remedy.

For technical claims we may ask you to work through reasonable troubleshooting steps with us first, such as confirming the product version, operating system and DAW or host and sending diagnostic information, so that we can identify and, where possible, fix the problem.

5. Situations not normally eligible

Except where mandatory law provides otherwise, we do not normally provide a voluntary refund solely because:

  • you changed your mind after the product was delivered, downloaded or activated;
  • the wrong product was purchased;
  • the system requirements were not checked before purchase;
  • you use an unsupported operating system, DAW, host or hardware configuration;
  • the product does not suit your personal workflow or preferences;
  • you no longer need the product;
  • you lack the equipment or knowledge required to use it;
  • a third-party operating system, DAW or host changed after your purchase; or
  • the software was a free beta and no payment was made.

These exclusions do not apply where the product is legally defective, misdescribed or does not conform to the contract. In those cases your statutory remedies apply in full (see sections 4 and 7).

6. EU and EEA withdrawal rights

If you are a consumer in the European Union or the European Economic Area, you normally have the right to withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days without giving a reason.

For digital content that is not supplied on a physical medium, such as downloadable software and licence keys, the right of withdrawal is lost once delivery or downloading begins only if, before delivery, you:

  1. expressly consented to the digital content being supplied immediately, during the withdrawal period; and
  2. acknowledged that you would lose your right of withdrawal as a result.

Simply downloading a product does not by itself remove the right of withdrawal in every case. Where this consent and acknowledgement were not obtained at checkout, or where mandatory law says the right continues, your statutory right of withdrawal is preserved and you may withdraw within the 14-day period by contacting us (section 8) or Lemon Squeezy.

The right of withdrawal is separate from, and does not limit, your rights concerning software that is defective, misdescribed or does not conform to the contract.

7. Request period

Voluntary refund requests under section 4 should normally be submitted within 14 days of purchase.

This voluntary period does not shorten any longer statutory period that applies to defects, non-conformity, unauthorised payments or other legal rights. For example, EU consumer law gives you conformity rights for digital content that last well beyond 14 days.

8. Request process

Send refund requests to vald.labs.lisbon@gmail.com. To help us find and assess your order, please include:

  • your name;
  • the email address used for the purchase;
  • the Lemon Squeezy order number;
  • the product;
  • the purchase date;
  • the reason for the request; and
  • for technical claims, relevant system information (operating system, DAW or host, product version) and the troubleshooting steps already tried.

Do not email full payment-card details. We never need them; payment information is handled by Lemon Squeezy.

Consumers exercising an EU or EEA right of withdrawal may use the same address and simply state that they wish to withdraw.

9. Approved refunds

Approved refunds are returned by Lemon Squeezy to the original payment method. Depending on your bank or payment provider, a refund may take up to 10 days to appear.

After a refund:

  • your licence to the product ends;
  • you must stop using the software and delete it from your devices; and
  • access to downloads, updates and support for that product may be removed.

A refund does not affect the ownership of music or other work you created with the software before the refund.
